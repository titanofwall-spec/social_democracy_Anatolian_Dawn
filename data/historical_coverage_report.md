# Cold War governments database — research draft

Coverage: 108 records (all palette entries excluding Turkey), 1972-01-01 through 1981-12-31 inclusive.

Curated entries: 81; unresearched entries: 27.
Entries with all three fields populated in all periods: 78 / 108.
Transition periods total: 316.
Verification-status counts: {'cross_checked_yearly_lists': 98, 'provisional': 169, 'dated_reference_needed': 22, 'unresearched': 27}.

**Do not ship this as a production historical database.** Date-specific transitions and government composition need full research and audit. A period marked `unresearched` is a coverage placeholder, not a claim that leadership was unchanged. Even `cross_checked_yearly_lists` identifies a broad cross-check, not exhaustive exact-date verification.
