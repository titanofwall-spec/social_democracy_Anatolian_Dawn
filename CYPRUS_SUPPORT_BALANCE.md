# Cyprus military support balance

Military Resources are shown at the top of the right-hand Cyprus tab, which opens on entering Cyprus. Starting resources are 12 + round(28 × average land/naval/aerial strength), using branch strengths clamped to 0–1. Each new day replenishes round(4 × average strength), on both normal day skips and operation decisions. At the default 0.4 branch strengths, this gives 23 starting resources and 2 per day.

| Force | Action | Cost | Next-roll bonus |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Land | Reinforce troops | 4 | +2 |
| Land | Deliver weapons and supplies to TMT | 7 | +3 |
| Air | Provide close air support | 4 | +2 |
| Air | Deploy airborne reinforcements | 7 | +3 |
| Naval | Provide naval fire support | 4 | +2 |
| Naval | Interdict opposing supply routes | 7 | +3 |

The total queued bonus is capped at +6. Each of the six actions has one cooldown across all six districts: used on 20 July, it is available again on 23 July. Actions neither advance the date nor award leverage. The bonus is consumed exactly once by the next valid Atilla I roll and scores remain capped at 80. Saved results record baseScore and supportBonus separately. Military strength requirements and leverage rewards are unchanged.

Support can be prepared from 15 July and used through the final Atilla I roll on 24 July. After completion, controls explain that no upcoming Atilla I roll is available. British sovereign base areas remain visible but are not selectable Cyprus districts. Missing support fields in old saves are initialized without resetting existing resource balances.

The shared map and inline force menus accompany Cyprus events and their decisions, preserving the current event while support is selected. Both normal and Easy layouts use the same controls.
