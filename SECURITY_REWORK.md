# Internal Security rework

Removed the assistant-rewritten replacement cards Investigating Political Violence, Preparing an Economic Programme, Constitutional Reform, and Rights and Equal Treatment. Original Turkish party and ministry cards remain.

Grey Wolves and Raiders bans require at least 35% aggregate far-right force strength; Raiders must have formed. A ban removes 40% of current membership and 35% of current militarization, retaining a banned underground group. Later prosecutions remove up to 20,000 members and 0.03 militarization. Revenge Brigades first require investigation, then allow the same repeat prosecutions. Membership and militarization cannot become negative.

Four separate underground investigations expose the clandestine network. ÖHP starts at 100%; each clampdown removes 20 points, with a 20% residual floor. Its strength scales only positive existing far-right membership and militarization growth. At 100%, original historical game growth is exactly unchanged. Economic-health-driven losses are unchanged. The displayed strength appears below the far-right groups.

The existing American embargo action records a funding request if CHP is in government when it occurs. The 5 February 1975 funding event then appears in normal play, whether or not investigations are complete. Clampdowns require four investigations; a funding request must be read before an embargo-related clampdown is available. Without an embargo, four investigations directly unlock the ministry action. Event funding costs one budget and preserves the existing strength.

Police loyalty improvement is +0.2, recruitment +16 thousand, and weaponry +0.2 militarization for the existing one-budget cost. Loyalty and militarization are capped at 1; the recruitment loyalty penalty remains -0.05.
