# Desk, menus and card availability

All difficulties use four drawn-card slots. Cabinet and Municipalities occupy permanent fifth and sixth desk positions; they are excluded from the leadership panel and random government deck. Cabinet requires a CHP majority government. Municipality eligibility retains its control and cooldown requirements, and its return button restores the unused action and timer without advancing time. Each desk entry clears the previous scene's display.

Municipalities displays the most recently recorded local election's CHP-controlled provinces, with all other provinces gray. If no local election has been recorded, the map remains gray and identifies that absence. Local election result screens and map tabs no longer show a parliament diagram.

Politics groups Republican Senate and National Assembly under TBMM. CHP leads the assembly list, remaining parties follow inline, and independent entries appear last as Ind in their respective colors. Main no longer displays assembly composition or last-election vote shares. It now includes the next Senate election below the next municipal election.

New games start Kurdish Political Consciousness at 7%, Radicalism at 2%, and Paramilitarism at 0%. Existing political progress is not reset. Kurdish Rights labels Economic Aid as Eastern Development and adds Crime in the East. The restored original action starts at crime stage 5, requires police loyalty of 50% and effective police force of 55, and reduces the stage by one per operation. Stages display as 5 Rampant, 4 Clamping Down On, 3–1 Increasingly Weakened, 0 Safety Enforced. The original police militarization cost and Kurdish CHP support reward remain.

KMD prosecutions have four steps. Its card's penalties scale to 100%, 75%, 50%, 25%, then 0%; at completion neither the KMD card nor a further usable prosecution remains. Coalition Affairs is unavailable under a CHP majority. Generic card availability excludes navigation and declining to act; it also removes cards with no remaining action from existing hands. Advisor selection remains outside that filter.

Removed cards: Building a People's Party, its programme-publicity follow-up, The Democratic Left Movement, and Social Welfare. Compiled scenes are regenerated, rather than leaving hidden selectable copies.

Province Other tooltips use the existing body-level tooltip layer above both dialogs, with white text in dark mode. Demographic poll lists begin on a new line after the demographic heading. Previous and next music buttons match the original music toggle, navigate the current playlist, and preserve mute state. A one-track scripted playlist is not interrupted.

Verification includes the full regression suite, eight focused desk/menu scenarios, and Chrome checks of the six-slot desk, ownership map, refundable return, relocated compositions, local-election display, tooltip position/color, and music navigation.
