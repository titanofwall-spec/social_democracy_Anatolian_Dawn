# Cyprus Military Resource balance

The average of Land, Naval and Aerial Strength selects the same seven tiers used by the Defense dashboard. Resource accumulation starts on July 15. Actions remain locked until the first July 20 operation scene opens.

| Tier | Initial | Daily | Total available before July 25 actions |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Horrible | 8 | 0 | 8 |
| Decrepit | 11 | 1 | 21 |
| Poor | 22 | 2 | 42 |
| Adequate | 25 | 3 | 55 |
| Sufficient | 29 | 4 | 69 |
| Good | 44 | 9 | 134 |
| Excellent | 54 | 12 | 174 |

These totals include ten daily gains from July 15 to July 25. Completing all 12 distinct actions costs 66; completing them twice costs 132. Good retains 2 and Excellent retains 42 after the 24-action schedule, before income for July 26. Sufficient retains 3 after one complete set. Adequate can perform a partial set: an eight-action mix of four cheaper and four dearer actions costs 44, leaving 11; all twelve still cost more than its 55-point budget.

Through July 25, Decrepit has a strict three-action limit and Poor a strict six-action limit. Horrible is limited to two. Adequate and Sufficient may use each action once during this period. Good and Excellent can repeat actions after the usual three-day cooldown. These extra tier restrictions end on July 26; the cooldown continues.

The pending bonus cap is +10. Four actions (two +2 and two +3) can be used per day across July 20–22, then repeated on July 23–25. The next roll consumes the bonus once; the actual roll result is still capped at 80. Existing saves retain their resources rather than being credited a second starting pool. New campaigns receive the new starting budgets; missing early action counts are conservatively seeded from recorded cooldowns.
