# Municipal development

Saved in municipal_development with action counts and half-month timestamps. Old saves begin at the lowest development levels; political control and local election archives remain unchanged. Rendering is read-only.

The permanent sixth card retains its existing three-month overall cadence and refundable return. Project-specific cooldowns use calendar months: 12 ticks for six months, 8 for four months. Opening the card does not advance project counters.

Specified programmes: infrastructure 4 rounds / 6 months; regularization 8 / 6 months; transport and industry 6 / 4 months. The two follow-ups unlock only at completion and are one-time endpoints. Nothing deletes population or neighborhoods.

Provisional details where the request gives no numbers: migration restrictions have two rounds with six months between rounds; ordinary actions advance one stage per completion; libraries and bakeries have two rounds each and contribute jointly to the three Public Services advances. Ordinary actions retain existing resource costs and support effects, roads cost one resource. New Gecekondu actions have no resource cost or additional electoral/economic effects. Planning advances from cleared unsafe construction to the apartment-district endpoint.

Run scripts/test-municipalities.js for exact cooldown boundaries, all levels, caps, pure rendering, legacy defaults and save roundtrip. Existing desk regression covers refundable return and permanent slots.
