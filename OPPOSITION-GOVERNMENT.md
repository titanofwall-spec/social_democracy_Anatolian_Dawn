# Non-CHP governments

Automatic ministry actions have been removed. Non-CHP cabinets no longer execute player policies, set their completion flags, or gain electoral credit through this feature. No passive ministry effects are implemented yet.

The News screen remains, with a neutral government update. Existing saved policy records remain readable; already-applied historical effects are preserved rather than guessed at and reversed. Old saves do not resume automatic actions.

The shared policy definitions remain in use by CHP player actions, preserving their costs and effects. Explicit non-CHP calls are rejected without mutations.
