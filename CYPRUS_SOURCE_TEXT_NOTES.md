# Military event descriptions and choice tooltips

The twelve decision-event descriptions and the July 30 ending excerpt use the attached source-passages manifest. Turkish book passages are translated into English, with their original extraction and PDF page preserved. UN excerpts preserve the report wording, with ellipses marking omitted words and links to the individual reports. No invented bridge paragraphs are inserted in the descriptions.

These are historical excerpts. They do not assert the player's alternate state. Decision actions and simulated roll outcomes remain game writing; no source is claimed for the hypothetical outcomes. The July 15–20 source readings were already PDF translations and remain unchanged. Existing original mod stories are preserved.

All twenty-five military decision choices use the existing choice-tooltip styling, deriving both the choice classification and the military requirement from the same action records used by the rolls. Labels no longer begin with Historical or Alternative. Strength requirements use the average of land, naval and aerial strength. Scoring, leverage, dates, support costs and cooldowns are unchanged.

The internet-sourced UN quotations are short excerpts, not complete reproduced paragraphs. Uploaded book excerpts can be translated at greater length. See CYPRUS_MILITARY_SOURCE_PASSAGES.json for exact provenance.
