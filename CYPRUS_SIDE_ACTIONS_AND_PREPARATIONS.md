# Side targeting, early-day routing and portrait overlays

Map selection now reads the red or blue position clicked on the supplied image. The six province polygons are no longer selectable. Both British base polygons remain excluded. Two labelled buttons provide keyboard-accessible Turkish/Greek selection. The selection is saved; image changes retain it.

Each side has two generic actions per force branch (12 total). Turkish-side actions reinforce, supply, entrench and coordinate landings. Greek-side actions scout, skirmish, conduct air reconnaissance, strike military positions, shell military positions and disrupt supplies. Costs remain 4 resources for +2 or 7 for +3, with a global three-day cooldown per action and the existing +6 next-roll cap. These are provisional generic actions.

The July 15 source reading advances to July 16. The July 16 source reading leads to the opposition meeting on the same day, then advances to July 17. The earlier military briefing on July 17 now precedes the London source reading. The last passage advances to July 18, and July 18–19 similarly advance automatically. The July 20 reading leads into that day's operation decision, without advancing past it. Domestic calendar updates retain their existing half-month boundary rule.

Portrait tooltips are displayed in a detached, fixed overlay above sidebar stacking contexts. The entire portrait-and-description popup scales to 75 percent and is positioned inside the viewport. Existing portrait selection and description contents are preserved.
