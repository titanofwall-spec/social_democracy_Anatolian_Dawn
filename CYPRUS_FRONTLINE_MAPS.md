# Supplied frontline map progression

The ten original PNG files are used unchanged. Their 4250 × 2573 canvas matches the existing SVG province overlay. Only the SVG background image changes; all six selectable provinces, selection highlighting, keyboard selection and military support controls remain active.

July 20–26 each use their own daily image. July 27–28 share their supplied image; July 29 retains that image because no July 29 artwork was supplied. July 30–31 share the image named 30–31 Temmuz, as confirmed by the user. August 1 and later retain the August 1 image. Before July 20 the existing map remains in use.

These are date-based historical artwork stages, not alternate-outcome territorial drawings. Rolls, scoring, resource costs and action cooldowns are unchanged. Loading an existing save derives the image from its Cyprus calendar without changing its saved state.
